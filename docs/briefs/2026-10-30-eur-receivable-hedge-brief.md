## The exposure

Our company expects to receive 4,500,000 euros in one year. Since we budget in dollars, what matters is how many USD those euros are worth. The euro amount is fixed, but the dollar amount is not since the dollar exchange rate will fluctuate between now and then.
## What a bad move costs
If the euro falls from $1.10 to $1.00 which is about a 9% fall, we would receive $4,500,000 instead of $4,950,000, leaving us $450,000 short of our budget, which could mean underfunding for necessary company resources, downsizing, or layoffs,
