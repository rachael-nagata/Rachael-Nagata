## The Exposure

Our company expects to receive 4,500,000 euros in one year. Since we budget in dollars, what matters is how many USD those euros are worth. The euro amount is fixed, but the dollar amount is not since the dollar exchange rate will fluctuate between now and then.
## What a Bad Move Costs
If the euro falls from $1.10 to $1.00 which is about a 9% fall, we would receive $4,500,000 instead of $4,950,000, leaving us $450,000 short of our budget, which could mean underfunding for necessary company resources, downsizing, or layoffs,
## The Three Hedge Tools
A forward is a contract today where you know exactly what you will get because it locks in the exchange rate for our euros in one year, but the downside is that if the euro gets stronger over that time period, you will not benefit. For example, if the locked rate were 1.10 you would receive $4,950,000 no matter what happens to the euro.

A money-market hedge lets us borrow euros and convert them to dollars. The benefit is that it gives the same certainty as a forward. The downside is it is complex and uses up our ability to borrow.

A put option protects us by giving us insurance. The benefit is protection from a fall while we still keep any gain if the euro rises, but the downside is we pay a fee regardless.
## What I am Assuming
We assume a bank will give us a forward or option at a fair price. We assume the customer pays in full. We assume that the euros arrive on time in one year. If I had more time, I would test that the customer pays in full first. 
## Hypothesis 
We expect the forward to leave us with the most dollars if the euro falls below about 1.10, because the bank still pays us the higher rate we agreed on. We expect unhedged to win if the euro rises above that, because the forward makes us give up the gain. This is assuming the forward rate is close to today’s rate.
## How I Would Know I Was Wrong 
I would be wrong if the model showed unhedged leaving us with fewer dollars than the forward when the euro rises above 1.10, if the bank’s forward rate were much higher than we assumed.
