## Where things are
- capabilities/   what I can do, each backed by an example
- docs/briefs/    written BEFORE work: scope + hypothesis
- analysis/       findings and figures
- data/           sourced inputs, with provenance
- prompt-log.md   top level, log of my AI sessions

## Naming
- If you are not sure which folder a file belongs in, ask me. Do not choose.
- Never invent a path or filename. I will give you the exact one.

## How I work
I want things explained in simple easy to understand language, please provide detail and simple steps to follow. Please provide examples whenever possible, and please do your best to keep it short and simple
- When you are uncertain, say so and say what would resolve it.

## What you may and may not draft
- You MAY explain, critique, debug, quiz me, and draft mechanical files.
- You MAY NOT write my briefs, analyses, memos, reflections, or bio.
- Every statistic you give me is a draft until I verify it against a source.

## Never include in a model or in this repo
 HEMIC claims files, medical reports and hearing decisions, Guidewire records, AMA sponsor contacts and information, Student Academic Center check-ins and student ID numbers. Also: no passwords or API keys, and no personal data about other people.
If I paste something like this, stop and tell me.

## Commits
Descriptive messages: what changed and why. Never just "update".

## Prompt log
After any session that changed a file, add one entry to prompt-log.md: date, what I asked, what you produced, what was wrong, how it was caught. Never edit past entries.
